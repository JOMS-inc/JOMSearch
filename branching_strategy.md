**What version control stragety did you choose and how did you actually do it/enforce it?**
We have decided to use GitFlow as our branching strategy. We where already losely following the structure but have now adopted "develop" that serves as an integration branch for features. Besides that, we will integrate a naming convention for our branches, being: **feature/, release/, hotfix/, bugfix/**.

**Why did your group choose the one you did? Why did you not choose others?**
We already worked with a similar stragety, so the transition would be easier and more clean. The reason we didn't choose others is convenience and simplicity.
